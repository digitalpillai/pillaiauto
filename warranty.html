FAQs

Warranty questions, answered plainly

What does 70% battery health mean?
Battery capacity naturally reduces with use. Eight70™ assures that your pack retains at least 70% of its original capacity within the warranty period — otherwise it's covered.
Is the rest of the scooter covered too?
Yes — the vehicle carries its own standard warranty separate from the battery cover. We'll share the current terms for your chosen model at the showroom.
Does fast charging hurt the battery?
Ather's charging systems and battery-management software are designed to manage fast charging safely within the warranty framework.
Can I check my battery health?
Yes — ask for a battery health report at our Perungudi service centre during any visit.
Warranty terms, durations and BaaS availability are set by Ather Energy and may vary by model, variant and purchase date. We'll confirm the exact terms applicable to your scooter in writing at purchase.
