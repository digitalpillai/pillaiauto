Questions

Your questions, answered.

What's the real difference between the 450X and 450S?

The 450X is the more capable of the two — a bigger optional battery for up to 161 km IDC range, a full touchscreen dashboard, and Warp mode for quicker acceleration. The 450S covers the essentials at a lower price: solid range, Bluetooth, and turn-by-turn navigation on a colour LCD screen. Most first-time buyers we see go with the 450X; budget-conscious daily commuters often prefer the 450S.

How long does a full charge actually take?

On a standard home charger, figure roughly 5h 45m for the 450X and 450 Apex, and about 7h 45m for the 450S — most owners just plug in overnight. On the road, Ather Grid fast-chargers can add around 15 km of range in just 10 minutes.

Can I extend the battery warranty beyond 3 years?

Yes — every 450 ships with a standard 3-year battery warranty, and you can upgrade to Ather's Eight70 add-on through AtherStack Pro for coverage up to 8 years. We can set this up for you at the time of purchase.

Do you offer test rides at home?

Yes. Walk into our Perungudi showroom on OMR, or call us to arrange a home test ride if you're anywhere across Chennai, Pondicherry, Chengalpet, or Chidambaram.

What financing options are available?

We offer zero down payment financing with interest rates starting from 3.99% and tenures up to 5 years, subject to lender approval. Exact EMI depends on the variant and your city — our team can work out the numbers with you in-store.
