FAQs

Service questions, answered

How often does an Ather need service?
Follow the periodic schedule in your owner's manual — broadly an initial inspection followed by regular check-ups. Because there's no engine, visits are quicker and cheaper than petrol-scooter servicing.
Do you service scooters bought from other Ather dealers?
Yes. As an authorised service centre we support any Ather, regardless of where it was purchased.
Do you support customers in Pondicherry, Chengalpet and Chidambaram?
Yes — call us to plan a visit or ask about pickup options for your area.
Are software updates free?
AtherStack over-the-air updates are delivered by Ather to your scooter; we verify and install the latest software during every service visit.

Due for a service?

Book a slot this week — quick turnaround, genuine parts, and a scooter that comes back better than it left.
