FAQs

Charging questions we hear every day

Can I charge at home without any special wiring?
Yes. The portable charger works on a standard 5A three-pin socket — the same kind you'd use for a home appliance. A dedicated socket at your parking spot is ideal.
How long does a full home charge take?
Roughly overnight. On the 450 series, 0–80% takes about 4.5–5.5 hours depending on the variant and charger; the Rizta charges fully in around 8.5 hours on the standard charger.
What does fast charging cost?
Ather Grid pricing is per-unit and shown in the app before you plug in. We'll walk you through it during your showroom visit.
Is the battery safe in Chennai rains?
Yes — the battery pack is IP67-rated against dust and water ingress, and it's engineered specifically for Indian operating conditions.
