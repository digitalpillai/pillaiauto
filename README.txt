PILLAI AUTO (ATHER OMR) — 15-Page Dealership Website
=====================================================

HOW TO USE
1. Open index.html in any browser to preview locally.
2. To publish, upload the whole folder (including the assets/ folder)
   to any web host (Hostinger, GoDaddy, Netlify, etc.).

ADDING IMAGES & VIDEOS  (IMPORTANT)
Every dashed green box on the site is an image/video slot.
Do NOT copy images or videos from atherenergy.com — that content is
Ather Energy's copyrighted material. As an authorised dealer, request
the official DEALER MARKETING ASSET KIT from your Ather dealer portal /
area sales manager, or shoot photos of your own showroom and stock.
To insert an image, replace a <div class="ph">...</div> block with:
   <img src="assets/your-image.jpg" alt="Ather 450X" style="border-radius:14px">

FORMS
The test-ride and contact forms are front-end demos. Connect them to
your enquiry system (e.g., a form service like Formspree, or your CRM)
so submissions reach you.

UPDATING FACTS
Prices, offers and variants change often — the site deliberately says
"call for today's quote." Review spec figures against your latest
Ather dealer bulletin before going live.
