About the brand we represent

Why Ather?

Founded in 2013 by Tarun Mehta and Swapnil Jain, Ather Energy built India's first truly performance-focused electric scooter and pioneered the Ather Grid fast-charging network. With software-defined scooters that improve over the air, IP67-rated batteries engineered for Indian conditions, and the industry-leading Eight70™ warranty, Ather sets the standard we're proud to sell and service.

  • India-designed, India-built electric scooters
  • Ather Grid: 6,000+ fast-charging points in 370+ cities
  • AtherStack software with over-the-air updates
  • Eight70™: 8-year / 80,000 km battery warranty with 70% health assurance
